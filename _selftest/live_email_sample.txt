主题：关于货物单证核验结果的通知及整改要求

尊敬的[供应商/货代名称]：

您好！

首先感谢贵公司一直以来对我司的支持与配合。近日，我司对贵公司提供的货物单证进行了核验，发现存在以下问题，特此通知并要求贵公司予以整改：

1. 缺少必需单证：原产地证书（Certificate of Origin）未提供。
2. 货物描述一致性：货物描述与多数单证不一致。出口报关单（Export Customs Declaration）显示为“卫浴陶瓷制品”，与基准“陶瓷卫浴洁具”的语义相似度为0.55。
3. 件数（箱数）一致性：多数单证显示为480件，而出口报关单（Export Customs Declaration）显示为475件。
4. 毛重一致性（容差1%）：装箱单（Packing List）显示毛重为12500kg，超出商业发票（Commercial Invoice）基准12300kg的1.63%，超出1%容差。

为确保货物顺利出口，请贵公司于2个工作日内完成以下整改并回复确认：

1. 补充提供原产地证书（Certificate of Origin）。
2. 确认货物描述是否为“陶瓷卫浴洁具”，若不一致，请更正并重新提供相关单证。
3. 确认件数是否为480件，若不一致，请更正并重新提供相关单证。
4. 确认毛重是否在1%容差范围内，若超出，请更正并重新提供相关单证。

期待您的回复，并感谢您的配合。

此致
敬礼！

[发件人姓名/公司]

=== English ===
Subject: Notification and Rectification Requirements for Verification of Goods Documents

Dear [Supplier/Forwarder Name],

First of all, thank you for your continuous support and cooperation with our company. Recently, we have conducted a verification of the goods documents provided by your company and found the following issues, which we hereby notify and require your rectification:

1. Missing essential document: Certificate of Origin is not provided.
2. Consistency of goods description: The goods description in the Export Customs Declaration (Export Customs Declaration) is "Sanitary Ceramic Products", which has a semantic similarity of 0.55 to the benchmark "Ceramic Sanitary Fixtures".
3. Consistency of quantity (number of cartons): Most documents show 480 pieces, while the Export Customs Declaration (Export Customs Declaration) shows 475 pieces.
4. Consistency of gross weight (tolerance of 1%): The gross weight in the Packing List (Packing List) is 12500kg, which exceeds the benchmark 12300kg in the Commercial Invoice (Commercial Invoice) by 1.63%, beyond the 1% tolerance.

To ensure the smooth export of the goods, please complete the following rectifications and reply for confirmation within 2 working days:

1. Provide the Certificate of Origin (Certificate of Origin) in supplement.
2. Confirm whether the goods description is "Ceramic Sanitary Fixtures", if not consistent, please correct and resubmit the relevant documents.
3. Confirm whether the quantity is 480 pieces, if not consistent, please correct and resubmit the relevant documents.
4. Confirm whether the gross weight is within the 1% tolerance, if beyond, please correct and resubmit the relevant documents.

We look forward to your reply and thank you for your cooperation.

Yours sincerely,

[Sender's Name/Company]
